    AQA practical skills

    Required Practicals 9, 10 and 11

    Collect simulated readings, build the correct graph, identify control variables and practise evaluation points. The Advanced Lab adds virtual apparatus, live traces and uncertainty analysis.

    RP9 · Charge and discharge of a capacitor

    Measure the falling pd during discharge, then use a log-linear plot to determine the time constant RC.

    Evaluation focus: use a high-resistance voltmeter/data logger, discharge fully between runs, keep R and C fixed, collect enough readings and use ln(V) against t to obtain −1/RC from the gradient.

    Results and analysis

    t / sV / Vln(V/V₀)
    Collect at least five readings.

    RP10 · Force on a current-carrying wire

    Investigate F = BIL by changing current, wire length in the field or flux density.

    Evaluation focus: zero the balance, keep the wire perpendicular to B, ensure only the measured length lies in the uniform field and allow the current to settle before reading.

    Results

    I / AL / mB / TF / N
    Collect a range of readings.

    RP11 · Flux linkage and coil angle

    Investigate how NΦ varies with the angle between the coil normal and the magnetic field.

    Evaluation focus: define the angle clearly, keep coil area and field strength fixed, repeat angle settings and compare NΦ with cosθ.

    Results

    θ / °cosθNΦ / Wb turn
    Collect readings across 0–180°.
